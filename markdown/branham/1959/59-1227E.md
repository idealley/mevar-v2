173 Abraham met God, and God said, “You’re going to have a baby by Sarah.” And him seventy-five, and her sixty-five. Abraham called everything that wasn’t, was con-…was contrary to that, as though it wasn’t. He walked as if he seen God, and he—he believed.

174 He’d call anything that said that he wasn’t, any reasonings, anything else that…The doctor might have said, “Why, Abraham, you’re too old to do that.” That don’t make a bit of difference what the doctor said, what anybody else said, what his own mind said, what anything else said. God was right, and he stayed with it.

183 The ambulance come, and they had a dead man laying in the back of the ambulance. Said, “I can’t take her.”

184 And so the farmer said, “Well, send somebody else, quick. The woman is a dying.” I walked up there. And I said, “Lady, just a minute.”
