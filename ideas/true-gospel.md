## 2. Ray Comfort & the True Gospel

Ray Comfort (The Way of the Master) presents a very clear, Scripture-grounded explanation of the gospel that many professing Christians find uncomfortable.

### Core Points
- **The Law + Gospel method**:  
  Use the Ten Commandments to show people they are guilty sinners (Romans 3:19-20; 7:7).  
  Then present the good news: Jesus paid the full penalty on the cross.

- **Why many “Christians” get angry**:
  - Their assurance is often based on a one-time decision (“I accepted Jesus into my heart”).
  - The biblical gospel demands **ongoing repentance**, fruit, and self-examination (Matthew 7:21-23; James 2:14-26; 2 Corinthians 13:5).
  - The Law does its job — it convicts and kills self-righteousness (Galatians 3:24). The natural heart hates being exposed.

- **Salvation is God accepting us — not the other way around**:
  - The phrase “accept Jesus into your heart” **never appears in the Bible**.
  - The Bible presents it the opposite way: God must accept us on His terms (John 6:37, 44; Ephesians 1:4-6; Romans 8:29-30).
  - True salvation produces repentance, faith that works, and fruit (Mark 1:15; Acts 2:38; Ephesians 2:8-9 + James 2).

**Summary**:  
Ray Comfort is simply doing what Jesus, John the Baptist, and the apostles did: show sin by the Law, then point to the cross. The anger often reveals that the message is hitting the heart exactly as Scripture says it should.
